About Us

Who we are

TISA — the Technology Innovators Students' Association — is a student-led community at UICT built on one belief: technology should be reliable, accessible, and within reach of everyone. We turn bold ideas into real-world solutions, preparing our members to lead, compete, and thrive in an ever-evolving technological world.

From hands-on builds to shared learning and mentorship, everything we do is aimed at one thing — preparing our members to lead in an ever-evolving technological world.

Motto: Reliable Technologies For Generation
What We Offer

Programs & opportunities

Four pillars that shape every TISA member's journey — from first-year beginner to industry-ready innovator.

🛠️

ICT Hub Access

Approved project teams get hands-on access to the TISA space at the UICT Hub — equipped for real software and hardware builds, prototyping, and collaboration.

🎓

Technical Training

Structured workshops across web development, AI, mobile apps, cloud, data science and more — designed to move you from theory to shippable projects fast.

🤝

Mentorship Program

Get paired with senior students, alumni and industry mentors who guide your projects, review your code, and help you grow into a confident, competitive innovator.

🚀

Career Development

Portfolio reviews, CV clinics, interview prep, internships, and networking with partner companies — we help you land your first role before you graduate.

Events & Activities

What we get up to

Activities that promote learning, skill development, networking, and collaboration — planned with our members and executed together.

Workshops & Seminars

Practical, instructor-led sessions on emerging tech tools and industry practices — from beginner bootcamps to advanced deep-dives.

Hackathons

Time-boxed build sprints where teams race to prototype solutions, sharpening problem-solving and teamwork under pressure.

Guest Lectures

Insights and Q&A sessions with industry professionals, alumni, and tech leaders who've walked the path you're on.

Innovation Challenges

Themed challenges aligned with the yearly TISA-U theme — one software project and one mechanical project per year.

Partnerships

Our partner network

We work with industry, technology, community, and university partners to open doors for our members.

Primary Host & Supporter

UICT

Our Home Institution

UICT is TISA's host institution and most valued ally. Everything we do — our projects, events, and mentorship — happens within the academic framework, values, and vision of the Uganda Institute of Information and Communications Technology.

Principal Innovation Ally

ICT-Hub

Where Ideas Become Real

The ICT-Hub is TISA's primary innovation collaborator. It provides the space, tools, and technical support that allow our members to build, prototype, and ship real-world technology — one of the most important pillars behind everything TISA achieves.

What we're working toward

Our Objectives

  • To translate students' technology innovative ideas into real-world problem-solving projects.
  • To create self-employment amongst students even before accomplishment of their studies.
  • To expose students to the current and future technology tools as applied in the evolving technological world.
  • To promote unity amongst current students, alumni & all UICT stakeholders.
  • To produce self-motivated citizens who have the will and ability to actively compete in the evolving technological world.
  • To make UICT the leading Technology Institute, worldwide.
What we stand on

Our Core Values

  • Believing the Almighty God for the success of our innovations, worldwide.
  • Trusting ourselves that we can make it.
  • Ensuring qualitative, reliable and easily accessible technologies at all times.
  • Proper time utilization and management.
  • Effective and efficient utilization of the available resources.
