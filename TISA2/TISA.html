About Us

Who we are and what we stand for

TISA is a student-led community dedicated to technology, innovation, and collaboration. We bring together students passionate about software, hardware, AI, design, and more.

Our mission is to equip members with practical skills, real-world experience, and a strong network to thrive in the tech industry.
